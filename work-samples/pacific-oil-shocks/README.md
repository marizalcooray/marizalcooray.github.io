# Pacific oil shocks

Simulation model and pass-through estimates for the impact of the 2026 oil price shock on inflation and GDP growth in Tonga, Fiji and Samoa.

- `Oil_shocks_model_v05.xlsx`: Excel simulation model, with methodology notes
- `pass_Tonga_v2.do`, `pass_Fiji_v0.do`, `pass_Samoa_v0.do`: Stata pass-through estimates by country
