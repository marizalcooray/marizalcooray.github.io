Mariza Cooray

Economist · M.A. International and Development Economics, Yale University (2027)

Research Policy writing CV Contact

I am an Economist and a graduate student at Yale University’s Department of Economics. I am interested in spatial trade research and research on poverty and inequality mechanisms. My interest stems from my work experience prior to Yale. I worked closely on data from small island economies and experienced working with large and incomplete datasets and strangely, enjoyed doing so.

I have been with the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and worked on nowcasting GDP for small-island economies as well as measuring impacts of the oil price hikes in 2026 on these economies. Prior to that, I worked through ADB with the government of Papua New Guinea to strengthen fiscal transparency through enhanced macroeconomic modelling. One of my favourite experiences has been to teach (and learn from) peers in the Pacific and in Indonesia.

Working papers

Tracking the Hidden Forces Behind Laos' 2022 Exchange Rate Crisis and Balance of Payments Instability
with Rolando Gonzales Martinez · arXiv:2503.13308 · March 2025
Uses a dynamic factor model to identify the latent drivers of the kip's depreciation and Laos' balance-of-payments instability.
Enhancing Poverty Targeting with Spatial Machine Learning: An Application to Indonesia
with Rolando Gonzales Martinez · arXiv:2503.04300 · March 2025
Adds spatial contiguity to proxy means testing using Indonesian household data (2016–21). Cuts exclusion errors from 28 to 20 per cent relative to standard machine learning models.
